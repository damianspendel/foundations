What the building stands on

  • Lean's type theory (the calculus of inductive constructions) is the ground floor. It is not ZF set theory; Part I builds a model of ZF sets inside it and proves the axioms of ZF as theorems.
  • Three axioms: propositional extensionality, soundness of quotients, and classical choice. Each headline theorem lists exactly which of them it uses.
  • Built-in numbers are earned, not assumed. ℕ and ℤ are first constructed from scratch and proved isomorphic to Lean's built-in versions; only then are the fast built-ins used.
  • One trusted shortcut: Lean's kernel evaluates natural-number arithmetic natively (via GMP) for speed. The from-scratch Peano construction does not rely on it.
